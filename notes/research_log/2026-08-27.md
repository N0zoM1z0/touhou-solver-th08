- Added a generic, pointer-normalized projection of each active enemy's
  opcode-128 effect-owner registry. Effect pointers are represented by stable
  EffectManager allocator slots; the record also retains source-defined death
  mode, death latch, no-death gate, callback subroutine, and both enemy flag
  words. Solver commits `b5940a4` and `cf9d4d6` preserve these diagnostics.
- The complete solver suite passes after both changes: 1,652 tests, 5 skipped,
  zero failures. The bounded Wine runs used the isolated TH08 prefix/display
  and removed their owned replay slot normally.
- The death-edge trace at replay 6,135--6,137 shows identical Linux/Wine boss
  state before the transition: mode 2, latch false, no-death false, callback
  subroutine 45, owner count 6, and the same six effect slots. At 6,137 both
  consume the callback and set the latch. Old Linux alone clears the owner
  count and marks all six effect-ID-13 instances for fade; Wine retains them.
- The former lifecycle difference quantitatively explains the later RNG split.
  Linux removes the six effects after their 16-update fade, then allocator
  reuse creates six extra effect-ID-38 instances. Their initializers consume
  ten RNG words each, matching the exact +60 calls first visible at replay
  6,838.
