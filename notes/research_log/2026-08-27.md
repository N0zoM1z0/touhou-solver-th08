## The first uncapped Easy route exposes a lifecycle witness bug

- The native generic planner completed all of Stage 1 through manager frame
  18,643 with 22,667 planned epochs, zero hit edges, and zero Bomb input. Mean
  and p95 total solver cost were 22.47 and 36.99 ms. Peak observed occupancy was
  254 bullets and 23 enemy bodies; minimum immediate clearance was 8.19 pixels.
- At bridge epoch 22,801 GameManager entered loading state 1 for Stage 2. The
  driver returned mask `0x0005`; epoch 22,802 observed the source input globals
  reset to zero and the old unconditional echo assertion disconnected. The
  compact 22 KiB failure report is
  `artifacts/runtime_reports/easy_linux_stage1_transition_failure_20260827.json`.
- Source commit `c38a92a` locates the reason in `Supervisor::OnUpdate`: ordinary
  epochs copy and refresh the two input globals, while a Supervisor state
  transition explicitly zeros both. Input echo is therefore authoritative only
  across adjacent ready observations in the same GameManager callback and
  route-stage lifecycle.
- The Easy route driver now enforces that domain, still fails closed for any
  within-stage delivery mismatch, and records cross-lifecycle differences as
  `input_echo_lifecycle_resets`. Five focused route-tool tests pass, including
  ready/loading and direct-stage-change cases. This is an infrastructure fix;
  the uncapped full route must be rerun before claiming completion.
- A nonblocking ten-second profile collected during Stage 1 attributes 77 of
  98 on-CPU samples to the baseline beam, 28 to binary32 movement stepping,
  ten to NumPy hazard evaluation, and eleven to complete pool capture. This
  identifies beam execution, rather than Wine sensing, as the dominant native
  Easy throughput cost; it is performance evidence, not yet a policy change.

- `main` was advanced to the retained Linux bridge checkpoint `b758455`, and

  `solver/linux-online-authority` at `e0c181e`: protocol v2 publishes after a

  `141cb3b69040626cf51e1837acad4600a409f332acd8ccfe1ae8cca5396c2995`.
- The Linux service now binds exact runtime/static ECL identity, the Stage
  1--5 no-scale-writer or Final-B source-trace scale authority, complete
  ordinary future projection, callback composition, geometry, context, and a
  one-frame 4 px/H80 corridor version. A real winning set reaches the local
  planner as named non-relaxable action authority; full remaining local
  projection coverage is required before future births enter the local pass.
- Static review first caught three authority defects before gameplay: the old
  eight-frame macro-action was unsound as a one-frame set, the flat wrapper
  could relax a named set, and repeated fast solves could postpone a pending
  policy forever. CE-0272 records the corrections and regressions.
- A final physical-clock audit caught a fourth defect before commit: policy
  staging/query still used `enemy_manager_frame` although wire delivery uses
  input epochs. **Observed in authored source:** GameManager flag bit `0x400`
  makes `EnemyManager::OnUpdate` return before its timer increment, while ECL
  callback 26 can freeze bullet motion/individual enemy timers without
  necessarily freezing that manager timer. **Implemented:** coherent roots now
  carry dialogue/freeze/input-epoch fields; two safe roots must establish equal
  positive input/manager deltas; policy manager frames are bound to explicit
  source input epochs; and context/gate/delta changes invalidate the complete
  asynchronous clock generation. Sixteen focused online tests include unequal-
  delta revocation/recovery and all native freeze gates.
- Authored priority order shows that player movement/focus and shot geometry
  precede enemy damage/ECL/aimed births, which precede bullet update/collision.
  A single action-independent hazard movie is therefore not a complete future
  world. The selected target architecture keeps a small immediate tier and a
  rolling background tier but makes both consume one flat source-order native
  transition kernel with branch-aware state and exact one-frame edges near the
  root.
- Synthetic one-frame H80 solves take about 0.053 seconds without moving AABBs
  and 0.34--0.52 seconds with 100--400, supporting asynchronous publication
  rather than per-frame global solve. Conversely, retained `8/12/8` foreground
  cost is 17.72/32.17 ms mean/p95 and its one-frame proof does not cover an
  arbitrary held deadline fallback. This is an explicit promotion blocker,
  not hidden by the non-blocking bridge.
- Complete post-dual-clock Linux discovery passes 1,686 tests with five
  conditional skips. The native source build and fixed-layout verifier pass.
  No game, Wine process, or physical trial was started.
